About Knowledge Hub

Knowledge Hub is the institutional repository of Nabadwip Vidyasagar College, maintained by the Central Library. It preserves and provides open access to the college's academic and institutional record — question papers, faculty research, quality-assurance documentation, and campus activity reports — for students, faculty, and the wider public.
